Who We Are

Planning from
first principles

1st Principles Planning is a multidisciplinary consulting firm specializing in urban planning and landscape architecture. Our team brings together over 70 years of collective experience working across municipal, private, and community-driven projects.

We take a holistic approach to every project — one that considers the interconnected needs of communities, municipalities, developers, and the environment. From a single development permit to an entire neighbourhood plan, we bring the same rigour and care.

Our philosophy is built on genuine public engagement and community consensus. We believe the best plans are those that give people a real voice in shaping the places where they live, work, and play.
